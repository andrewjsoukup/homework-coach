# Demo: three days with the Maple family

The Maple family is **fictional**. Nova (grade 3) and Pip (kindergarten) do not
exist; their homework pages in `homework/` are generated by
`make_homework.py`, with a tiny vector "handwriting" font and seeded jitter.
No real child's writing is involved, and no photo of a real worksheet. Every
page has a `*.truth.json` (what a careful reader should observe). The demo and
tests use that file as the vision response, so the demo runs offline and sends
nothing to any model provider.

| Child | Grade | Minutes/day | Items/packet | Schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nova | 3 | 15 | 6 | Mon–Sat 09:00 |
| Pip | K | 12 | 5 (adult reads directions) | Mon–Sat 09:00 |

Run it:

```bash
python demo/run_demo.py            # writes demo/out/transcript.md (isolated data dir)
uv run demo/make_homework.py       # regenerate the homework PNGs + truth files
```

`scenario.yaml` holds the machine-readable script. `tests/test_demo_scenario.py`
replays it through the real difficulty engine, so this page can't drift
from the code.

**Pip: "Count and Write"** (`pip_2026-09-14_counting.png`)

| # | What's on the page | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 5, 6 | 5 apples, 7 stars, 6 hearts, 8 suns | correct |
| 3 | 3 balls, numeral written **backwards** | correct count; reversal is normal in K (noted, not penalised) |
| 4 | 9 scattered fish, wrote **8** | incorrect: lost track in the largest scattered group |
| trace | 1–5 tracing strip | warm-up, not scored |

**Packet:** Nova gets arrays as the classroom target, plus repeated-addition
review, in *fresh formats*: no "rows of ___ = ___" copy of the worksheet.
Pip gets counting to 10 plus numeral-writing review, without the
scattered-pictures format from the homework.

**Evening feedback**

- Nova `arrays I I I · addition I S`: "Breezed through arrays. Needed a nudge on the second addition."
- Pip `counting I S N · numerals I I`: "Lost track counting the big group of 9."

**Engine:** Nova arrays **up** (support fades: `scaffold 1→2`), addition holds.
Pip numerals **up** (`scaffold 1→2`). Pip counting is **down**, but it already
has full support, so it stays at the floor with maximum scaffold (ten-frame,
smaller groups).

## Day 2: Tuesday 2026-09-15, no new homework

**Packet:** Nova's array items lose the printed dot grid (scaffold faded). Pip's
counting items stay on ten-frames, and his numeral tracing guides fade.

**Evening feedback**

- Nova `arrays I S I · addition I I`: "Without the dot grid she wanted help once."
- Pip `counting I I I · numerals S`: "Ten-frame helped a lot."

**Engine:** everything **holds**. One supported answer blocks a step-up (Nova
arrays, Pip numerals). Pip's three independent counts are one good day after a
"not yet", and the window still holds the Day-1 S/N. One good day isn't enough
to raise difficulty.

## Day 3: Wednesday 2026-09-16, new classroom topics

| # | What's on the page | Observation |
|---|---|---|

| 2–4 | 10/2, 15/3, 18/3 | correct |

**Pip: "More or Fewer?"** (`pip_2026-09-16_compare.png`)

| # | What's on the page | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 4 | 4 vs 6, 7 vs 3, 2 vs 8: circled the larger group | correct |
| 3 | 5 vs 5: circled the left group | incorrect: equal groups not yet "the same" |
| 5 | 9 vs 6: nothing circled | unattempted |

**Brief:** each child gets a dated **Current classroom anchor** for the new lesson.
Last week's topic is demoted to supporting review. The item budget stays the same.

**Packet:** Nova gets mostly sharing/division in rotating formats (draw groups,
story, related fact), plus array/addition review. Pip gets more/fewer comparisons,
with "same" introduced gently, plus counting review.

**Evening feedback**

- Nova `sharing I N S · arrays I · addition I`: "Subtracted instead of sharing on one."
- Pip `compare I N · counting I I`: "Didn't know what to do when the groups were the same."

**Engine:** the new topics are **down** at the floor (full support stays: picture
groups first, equal-groups examples). Nova's addition review is **up** (two clean
days). Pip's counting is finally **up** after two all-independent days.

| After Day 3 (scaffold, steps, representation, number_range) | |

What the demo shows: score only attempted work, tell slips from
misconceptions, record format as well as skill, move one dimension at a time
from parent evidence, and re-anchor when the class moves on.
